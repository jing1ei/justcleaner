JustCleaner · for macOS 11 and later

A quiet way to
keep a Mac clean.

One small icon in the menu bar. It finds the throwaway files your coding tools, browsers and apps leave behind, says in plain words what each one is, and clears them in a single click. Your music, projects, documents and settings sit behind rules that cannot be switched off.

It needs Apple's command line tools (swiftc), which any Mac used for coding already has — if they are missing, the installer says so and gives you the one command that adds them. About a minute, no password, and nothing installed but JustCleaner.app.

No compiler on that Mac?

Open JustCleaner on a Mac that already has it, choose More → Export app for another Mac… from its menu, and AirDrop the zip it leaves on the Desktop. Unzip, double-click Install JustCleaner.command. Nothing to download, nothing to compile.

What it does

  • Shows the number first. How much junk there is, split into categories written for a human, not a log file.
  • Cleans the safe part in one click. Disposable caches can be erased immediately; retained files stay recoverable.
  • Keeps a safety net. Everything else waits a few days in ~/.mactidy/quarantine and goes back with one click.
  • Leaves open apps alone. Caches belonging to recognized running apps are skipped; the menu names the app to close.
  • Never touches what matters. ~/Music, plug-ins, sample libraries, DAW projects, Documents, Desktop, photos, cloud folders, keys, app settings.
  • Explains itself. A full HTML report of every folder found, why it exists, and what would happen to it.
  • Answers from Terminal too. JustCleaner scan · clean · undo · doctor

Why it can be trusted

Check the safety rules

JustCleaner doctor runs the safety rules against a list of paths that must be refused — your music folder, a plug-in, a keychain — and shows the verdict for each one. If a single rule misbehaves, it says so and tells you not to clean anything.

Check with JustCleaner doctor

Nothing is deleted where it sits

Items are moved to the safety net first. Safe items may then be erased immediately; retained items can be undone until expiry. Only you can empty the Trash.

Reversible by design

It stays on your Mac

No account or telemetry. Scans and reports stay local; optional external tools may use their own network connections. No background daemon beyond the menu bar icon itself. The report is a plain file in your home folder.
